TAB 1 GUIDE

System Architecture & Workflow Order

BMW Show Assitant is designed with a sequential 7-step pipeline. Review the step order below to understand how data moves from your talent roster into shows, venues, schedule views, accounting ledgers, and automated snapshots.

1 Orientation

HELP & Pipeline Flow

Provides the master guide explaining module functions, how tabs interconnect, and how the show cost formula calculates. You can return here anytime by clicking Tab 1 or pressing 1.

Key Role: Master Guide & Calculations
2 Talent Roster

ACTORS

Add and subtract actors. Each actor stores their phone, email, emergency contact, specialty, and individual Show Rate ($) (defaults to $85.00, fully editable). Contains individual show logs.

Feeds into: Shows, Ledger
3 Client Directory

CUSTOMERS

Store venue directors and corporate clients before scheduling. Click "Booked Shows" on any customer card to review their lifetime booking record and billed production volume.

Linked to: Shows Production
4 Production Engine

SHOWS

Schedule productions. Select client, venue, date, time, and check off actors. The cost engine dynamically computes: Cost Base + (Actors × AAC) + Sum(Actor Rates) + Travel + Stay

Feeds into: Calendar, Accounting
5 Visual Schedules

CALENDAR

Inspect schedule density in Month and Week views. Clicking any show directly navigates to the SHOWS tab, automatically focusing and highlighting that production card.

Links into: SHOWS View
6 Financial Ledger

ACCOUNTING

Default list view from today forward displaying Show, Date, Actors, Cost, Fee, Net Margin, and Paid status. Interactive dropdown lets you change payment settlements with real-time KPI updates.

Summarizes: Revenues & Costs
7 BACKUP & Data Protection

Automatically takes a snapshot on opening to default.json and repeats every 5 minutes with a live countdown timer. Supports direct directory linkage via the File System Access API, custom folder/file naming, and instant JSON imports with auto-overwrite enabled.
